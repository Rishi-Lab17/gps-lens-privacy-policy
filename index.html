This Privacy Policy explains how GPS LENS: MAP CAMERA (“GPS LENS”, “the app”, “we”, or “our”) accesses, processes, stores and shares information when you use the app. Please read it before using the app.

1. Information and permissions the app uses

Location information. If you grant location permission, GPS LENS accesses your device location to provide location display, address and place lookup, map, route, camera-stamp and weather-related features. Precise location may be sent securely to Google Maps Platform services and OpenStreetMap services to provide mapping, geocoding, nearby-place and routing features. Location-related requests may also be sent to Open-Meteo for weather information. Approximate location and network-derived information may be processed by the advertising SDK as described below. You can manage location permission through Android settings; location-dependent features may not work when permission is denied.

Camera, photos and videos. The app uses the camera when you choose to capture media. Based on the app's current design, captured photos and videos are processed and saved on your device in the app's GPS LENS gallery folders. They are not uploaded by GPS LENS to a developer-operated media server. If you choose to share or export media using Android's system share sheet, the receiving app or service will handle the content under its own privacy terms.

Microphone and speech recognition. If you use voice-command features, the app uses Android speech recognition services. Audio/voice input is handled through the device's recognition service; the recognition provider may process information according to its own terms and privacy policy. GPS LENS does not operate its own voice-recording upload service.

Local app information. App preferences, local search history, cached information and app database records may be stored on your device to support app features. The app's current implementation excludes its local database and related journal files from the configured Android cloud-backup and device-transfer rules.

2. How information is used

Information and device permissions are used to operate the features you request, including displaying your location, resolving addresses, finding places, showing maps and routes, providing weather information, adding location context to captured media, supporting voice commands, maintaining local app settings and displaying advertisements.

3. Third-party services and sharing

GPS LENS uses third-party services to provide app functionality and advertising. When a feature makes a request to a provider, relevant information such as location, network information or device/app information may be transmitted to that provider.

  • Google Maps Platform — Maps, Places, Geocoding and Routes services. Relevant location and search/request information may be sent to Google to fulfil your requests. See Google Privacy Policy.
  • OpenStreetMap services — OpenStreetMap/Nominatim/Overpass data may be used for map, geocoding or place-related features. Requests can include location or search coordinates. See the OpenStreetMap Foundation Privacy Policy.
  • Open-Meteo — Weather requests may include geographic coordinates. See Open-Meteo terms and its applicable privacy information.
  • Google Mobile Ads (AdMob) — The app displays advertising, including banner, native, interstitial and app-open formats. The advertising SDK may collect or share data such as advertising identifiers or other device identifiers, approximate location or IP-derived information, ad interactions, and SDK diagnostics for advertising, measurement, analytics and fraud-prevention purposes. Advertising consent and choices are presented through Google's consent tools where required. See Google advertising information and Google Privacy Policy.
  • Android speech recognition and system sharing — If you use these Android-provided features, information is handled by the selected Android service or app under its own terms.

These providers may process information on systems they operate and may retain technical logs under their own policies. We do not control their independent retention practices. Please review the providers' policies for details.

4. Data stored on your device

Photos, videos, app preferences, local database records and other locally generated app information are stored on your device as described above. The app does not provide user account registration or a developer-operated account profile in its current version. The app's local-first media handling does not mean that information sent to third-party APIs or advertising services remains only on your device.

5. Data security

Network requests made by the app's integrated services are intended to use HTTPS encryption in transit. We take reasonable steps in the app's design to limit unnecessary collection and to keep media and app records local where the feature permits. No method of electronic transmission or storage is completely secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security.

6. Data retention and deletion

Locally stored media and app records remain on your device until you delete them, remove the app, or use an available in-app local-data deletion control. If the app version you use provides Settings → Privacy & Local Data → Delete Local Data, you can use that control to remove the local records and caches covered by the feature. Media saved to shared device storage may need to be deleted separately using your device's gallery or file manager.

GPS LENS does not currently provide user accounts or a developer-operated cloud profile from which account data can be deleted. Information already transmitted to Google, OpenStreetMap, Open-Meteo, an advertising provider, or another third-party service is subject to that provider's own retention and deletion practices. Contact the relevant provider for requests concerning data it independently holds.

7. Children's privacy

GPS LENS is a general-purpose utility and is not designed specifically for children. The app is not intended to knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided personal information through a third-party service used by the app, please contact the relevant service provider and contact us so we can review the concern.

8. Your choices

  • Manage location, camera and microphone permissions through Android settings.
  • Use Android's available advertising and privacy controls to manage ad personalisation and consent choices.
  • Delete local app data and media using the app's available controls and your device's storage tools.
  • Choose whether to use optional location-dependent or voice-enabled features by managing the relevant permissions.

9. Changes to this Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy when app features, SDKs, service providers or legal requirements change. The updated version will be published at this same URL with a revised effective date. Please review this page periodically.
